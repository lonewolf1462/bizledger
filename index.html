What Bizora is

One place to run the daily numbers of a shop: what you sold, what you spent, what is left on the shelf, and who owes you.

For shop owners

Keep clean sales, expense and debt records without spreadsheets, and see profit-oriented reports.

For managers

Watch performance across stores and cashiers, and keep an eye on stock.

For cashiers

A focused till screen. When sign-in is on, cashiers are limited to the POS.

Manager and cashier are different roles

Cashiers record sales and payments. Managers oversee the business. In the current build this split is enforced inside the browser app once the manager turns on sign-in.

Manager

Oversees the business

  • Full access to dashboard, inventory, expenses, debts and reports
  • Business performance view with store and cashier filters
  • Adds stores and cashiers, edits cashiers and resets PINs
  • Sets the manager PIN and turns sign-in on
  • Switches between stores when more than one exists
Cashier

Records sales and payments

  • Signs in with a PIN and lands on the Cashier / POS screen
  • Restricted to the POS; other pages redirect back to it
  • Tied to an assigned store
  • Cannot add or remove cashiers

Not built yet: a dedicated Manager Control Centre with fine-grained permissions, audited stock changes and refunds, and a live restock feed for managers. These are planned work, not features you can use today.

Privacy and data

  • Business records live in your browser storage on your device. This website does not receive them.
  • This site has no cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.
  • Clearing browser data or switching device loses records unless you exported a backup.
  • PINs in the prototype are not a substitute for real account security.
